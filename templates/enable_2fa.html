What is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account. When enabled, you'll need to enter a code sent to your email in addition to your password when logging in.

Benefits of 2FA:

  • Protects your account from unauthorized access
  • Prevents password theft
  • Secures sensitive medical information
  • Industry-standard security practice
